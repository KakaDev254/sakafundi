Cookie Policy

Last Updated: August 4, 2026

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and to provide information to the website owners.

How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

Cookie Type Purpose Duration
Essential Cookies Necessary for the Platform to function properly (e.g., login, session management) Session / Persistent
Preference Cookies Remember your preferences and settings (e.g., language, theme) 1 year
Analytics Cookies Help us understand how users interact with the Platform (e.g., Google Analytics) 2 years
Authentication Cookies Keep you logged in and maintain session security Session
Security Cookies Protect the Platform from fraud and abuse Session
Marketing Cookies Deliver relevant advertisements and measure campaign effectiveness 1 year
Types of Cookies We Use
  • Session Cookies: Temporary cookies that are deleted when you close your browser.
  • Persistent Cookies: Remain on your device for a set period or until you delete them.
  • First-Party Cookies: Set by SakaFundi directly.
  • Third-Party Cookies: Set by third-party services we use (e.g., Google Analytics, M-PESA).
Your Cookie Preferences

You can control and manage cookies in several ways:

  • Browser settings: Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies
  • Opt-out tools: You can opt out of analytics cookies using Google's opt-out tool
  • Cookie banner: You can adjust your preferences on our cookie banner
Managing Cookies in Your Browser
Chrome
Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies and other site data
Firefox
Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
Safari
Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
Edge
Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies
Third-Party Cookies

We use the following third-party services that may set cookies:

  • M-PESA: For processing payments
  • Cloudflare: For security and performance
  • Social Media: For sharing content (if enabled)
